# My Favorite Movie: La La Land

## Overview
*La La Land* (2016) is a musical romance directed by **Damien Chazelle**. It follows **Mia**, an aspiring actress, and **Sebastian**, a jazz pianist, as they chase their dreams in Los Angeles.

## Why I Love It
- Beautiful music and cinematography
- A bittersweet ending that stays with you
- Great chemistry between the leads

## Main Cast
| Character | Actor |
|-----------|-------|
| Mia | Emma Stone |
| Sebastian | Ryan Gosling |

## Themes
